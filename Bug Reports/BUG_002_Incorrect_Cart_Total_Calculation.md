# BUG-002: Shopping Cart Total Price Calculated Incorrectly

## Environment
- Browser: Google Chrome
- Operating System: Windows 11

## Severity
High

## Priority
High

## Preconditions
User has multiple products added to the shopping cart.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Add Product A (RM50) and Product B (RM30) into the cart.
2. Verify the cart total amount.

## Expected Result
The total price should display RM80.

## Actual Result
The system displays RM70 as the total amount.

## Status
Open
